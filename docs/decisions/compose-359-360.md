# Composing #359 and #360: order, method, arithmetic

Two sibling features were proved independently on the same corrected
baseline before either met the other:

    7fd6394 — c8d4723 (baseline: the contract watches the two page
              writers it never ran)
                ├── 53efb7e  #360 wide-grid-labels
                └── 8d5592f — fff01d2  #359 horizon-cardinal-marks
                                       and its repair

This record is the composition of the two: the order, the method,
what the first attempt found, and the arithmetic of the second.

## Order: #359 first, #360 on top

The order follows the dependency of the generated evidence, not a
preference between the features. #359's celestial-orientation wording
reaches about 210 derived paths and covers #360's entire 60-artifact
evidence surface; #360's reach is the narrower. So #359 establishes
the combined tree's generated baseline, and #360 regenerates its
affected evidence against that baseline. The other order would have
had the broad #359 regeneration overwrite part of #360's already
proved evidence, leaving the attribution to be reconstructed.

## What the first attempt found

The first composition experiment, from the frozen 8d5592f, measured
cleanly - the same classification as below - and then stopped at its
unit-suite gate: nine tests failed, and all nine reproduce on bare
8d5592f with no #360 content. The composition had introduced
nothing; the frozen #359 checkpoint's endgame had run focused tests
and both evidence contracts but never the full suite on its final
content, and nine tests still held facts the accepted feature had
changed. That checkpoint's promotion was discarded rather than
patched around, the repairs went to #359 itself as fff01d2 - seven
stale contracts updated by meaning, the LocalSky gate refined to
distinguish identity parameters from fidelity modes, and the
export-strings companion promoted once its whole delta was pinned
to the approved wording - and the composition was rebuilt from the
repaired head. An integration commit must not become a hiding place
for a sibling's defects.

## Method: a new experiment, not a merge of generated bytes

The integration branch starts at fff01d2. #360's semantic delta is
exactly three files - `EquatorialGrid.java`,
`EquatorialGridTest.java`, `ChartFurnitureTest.java` - and #359
(repair included) touches none of them, so the source composition is
conflict-free by construction. Neither sibling's committed generated
bytes were assumed to win anywhere: with both source changes
present, everything was regenerated from scratch by the same
four-pass route as #359's blast measurement (46 derived docs
writers; 6 build writers mapped to every promoted destination; 32
stdout reporters captured explicitly; 20 platform records captured
headless; a failed or empty generator refused, never read as
unaffected).
