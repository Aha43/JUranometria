# JUranometria articles

JUranometria is an atlas, but it is also an argument about how the sky should
be represented. These articles explain the astronomy and the architecture
behind that argument.

## Exploring the sky

- [From the celestial sphere to a page](from-celestial-sphere-to-page.md) —
  why detailed charts, wide overviews and celestial globes preserve different
  truths about the same sphere.
- [Where the Sun is](where-the-sun-is.md) — the four questions "where is the
  Sun" asks, where the atlas's numbers come from, how they are held to an
  authority, and how they reach the chart.
- [Which way the light falls](which-way-the-light-falls.md) — what the Moon's
  phase is, which way it faces on a chart and in your sky, and what a chart
  drawn at true scale can and cannot show of it.
- [Jupiter's little sky](jupiters-little-sky.md) — what the atlas draws of
  Jupiter and its four large moons, how the marks are to be read, and what it
  does not claim. Also in Norwegian: [Jupiters lille
  himmel](jupiters-lille-himmel.md).
- [The phases of Venus](the-phases-of-venus.md) — what the atlas draws of
  Venus, why a planet needs a phase on the chart, how to read the mark, and
  what it does not claim. Also in Norwegian: [Venus'
  faser](venus-faser.md).
- [Mercury, never far from the Sun](mercury-never-far-from-the-sun.md) —
  what the atlas draws of Mercury, why the drawing so often has to be a
  symbol, what the closer fields add, and what it does not claim. Also in
  Norwegian: [Merkur, aldri langt fra
  Solen](merkur-aldri-langt-fra-solen.md).
- [Saturn's rings on the page](saturns-rings-on-the-page.md) — what the
  atlas draws of Saturn, its rings and seven moons, how the rings turn
  over the years, how to read the figure and the marks, and what it does
  not claim. Also in Norwegian: [Saturns ringer på
  siden](saturns-ringer-pa-siden.md).
- [Mars on the page](mars-on-the-page.md) — what the atlas draws of Mars,
  Phobos and Deimos, why Mars is usually a symbol and sometimes a disc,
  how to read its phase and its two small moons, and what it does not
  claim. Also in Norwegian: [Mars på siden](mars-pa-siden.md).

## How JUranometria works

- [Language without translating the sky](../how/language.md) — how canonical
  astronomical identity, chart names, interface prose, accessibility and
  export remain separate.
- [Projections: technical companion](../how/projections.md) — the source and
  decision trail behind the projection article.

The binding architectural record remains [Technical
architecture](../architecture.md) and the measured decisions under
[`docs/decisions/`](../decisions/). The articles explain those records; they do
not replace them.
