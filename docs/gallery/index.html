JUranometria — a quiet star atlas

White paper, black ink, the fixed sky. The core chart is a chart; each module has a room of its own.

Core chart

The ordinary atlas as itself: stars sized by brightness, deep-sky symbols at catalogued size and orientation, constellation geography and the equatorial grid in quiet greys, and a title block that states exactly what the page shows.

  • The Andromeda Galaxy region
  • Orion at a glance
  • The Southern Cross
  • Around the celestial pole

On This Page

A removable module that lists everything the page carries — including what the chart does not draw — and lets a reader mark entries with working crosses. Interaction ink, never catalogue vocabulary.

  • Working crosses on the Andromeda page
  • The page's own inventory UI

Place and Time

A removable module that lays one observer's meridian, zenith and mathematical horizon over the fixed chart, for one frozen instant at one stated place. Nothing ticks.

  • The point overhead
  • Where the sky meets the ground
  • Three fields, three switches, two actions UI
