## The old transaction, and what it lost

The Chart Options dialog previewed every box on the chart, saved on OK,
and on Cancel, Escape or the close box wrote back the whole value it had
opened with. The chart keyboard (⌘/Ctrl-K, then a letter) changed and
saved the same options outside that transaction, and the dialog never
listened to its controller. Reproduced with real windows on #442 (the
log is in the decision record):

- **Cancel rolled the keyboard back.** It wrote the opening snapshot
  over a change the keyboard had already saved, so the chart and the
  store disagreed.
- **A stale box undid the keyboard.** Any later click rebuilt all
  seventeen options from the dialog's own boxes, re-applying the stale
  ones. OK then saved the undone value.

## The ruled semantics: shared immediate

Every accepted change is the chart at once and is saved at once,
whoever makes it: a box in either host, a letter on the keyboard, or
Restore Defaults. It was chosen because it is the only model in which a
panel left open always tells the truth about the chart. It was already
the keyboard's and the View switches' model.

- **Transactional dialog, rejected:** it would need Cancel to undo
  "only its own edits, unless something changed them since", a rule no
  reader can see.
- **Companion draft, rejected:** it would show a draft rather than the
  chart.

What shared immediate means in practice:

- **One field per box.** A box writes the controller's current value
  with its own field changed (`ChartSwitches.withChart`), never a whole
  value of its own.
- **Closing takes nothing back.** OK and Cancel are gone. Close, the
  close box and Escape close and revert nothing.
- **Restore Defaults asks first.** "Restore every chart option to the
  atlas defaults?" Cancel is the safe default. Confirmed, it is one
  accepted change, saved.

## One state, followed

`ChartOptionsController` holds the one current value.

- **Changes:** `accept(next)` applies and saves; `restoreDefaults()` is
  an accepted change.
- **Subscriptions:** `onChange` returns a `Subscription` (cancelling
  twice is once), and `subscribers()` counts them.
- **Gone:** `revertTo` and the snapshot.

Every presentation follows through one subscription. It re-ticks its
boxes and re-applies the two decided dependencies. A deep-sky family
or deep-sky labels is disabled while deep-sky objects are off, and
constellation names while figures are off. The child's remembered
choice is kept. Each subscription is released when the window holding
its host is disposed.

  It never touches the controller or the chart's stored options, and a
  test holds that.
- **Accessibility.** Each group heading is a focusable toggle whose
  accessible name says the subject and whether it is open. The boxes
  keep their names, access letters and explanations, which name their
  keyboard letters.
