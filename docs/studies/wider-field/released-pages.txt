# Released page renders, hashed

What this file is: a fingerprint of every page the atlas could draw
before the 42-degree sheet step was added, so that adding it can be
shown to have changed none of them.

The gate that chose the step (docs/decisions/printable-chart.md)
promised the 1-36 degree atlas would stay byte-identical. That is a
claim about pixels, and only pixels can settle it.

## How this was made

Each row carries three digests, and they are not interchangeable.

**marks** is what the renderer decided to draw and where: every star
and deep-sky mark, its subject, its centre and its reach.

**ink** is every shape it then drew or filled, with that shape's own
coordinates, its colour and its stroke width. It sees the grid, the
constellation boundaries and figures, and the chart ground - none of which
marks can reach. It stops at text, and the boundary is font metrics:
they place a label and decide whether it fits, so both where the labels
are and which ones there are may legitimately differ between machines.

Both of those are arithmetic, so they hold on any machine, and the test
checks them everywhere. **pixels** is the rasterised page - every label the
other two leave out, and every pixel of everything they cover. It depends
on the font stack and the JDK's own 2D pipeline, so it is an oracle only
on the exact platform named below - OS and version, architecture, and JDK
build - and the test skips it out loud anywhere else rather than
pretending.

Recorded on: `Mac OS X 26.5.2/aarch64/Homebrew 21.0.11`

Every released field step, at four centres that exercise the cases
the atlas treats differently - the M31 default, Orion on the
equator, a near-polar page, and the RA seam - on both chart grounds,
900 x 700, stars to V 8.0. Each page is rendered through the
production SceneAssembler and ChartRenderer, and both digests are
SHA-256, recorded to their first eight bytes.

Generated from commit 9ffc9e3, the merge of PR #288 - the last
commit before the step was added, and the code released as 1.9.0.
juranometria.tool.WiderFieldStudyMain regenerates the same table
from whatever is checked out; a row that no longer matches is a
released page that has changed.
