# Saturn on the chart: production pages

Sprint 53, issue #579 - the figure, the first of #579's two pull requests under the owner's rulings on #578. Each page is the production `ChartComponent` over the bundled catalogue with the Saturn module attached and switched on: Saturn's globe and rings at their true size where the rings' major axis is at least the 6 px minimum, otherwise the 6 px cartographic symbol; the rings' far half behind the globe and the near half over it; a line when edge-on; dimmed when the unlit face is seen; dimmed below a drawn horizon. The closer fields are reached as the application reaches them, through the controller's zoom in at the floor. Regenerate with `make saturn-on-the-chart-study`.
