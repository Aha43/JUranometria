# The Sun and Moon tables in the Controller, measured before they move

Sprint 42, issue #457. Generated by
`juranometria.tool.SolarSystemControlsStudyMain` from the compiled application.

Every mock-up holds the production table content - the same `SolarTableDialog.content`
the dialogs hold, over `PlaceAndTimeSheetMain`'s Oslo observer at its frozen instant - with
its controls lifted into the arrangements compared, and the production companion
section heading. Section and group titles, the proposed *Show on chart* box and the menu
rows use production words where they exist and study words where they do not. How tall
and wide the words are drawn is one machine's answer, in `platform.md` beside this.

## What the two dialogs hold

Read from `SolarTableDialog.content` for each body, in `en`: the controls in the
order a keyboard meets them, then the columns. The "state" column is the study's:
**observer** is Place and Time's, read on demand; a **draft** is the reader's typed
query; a **result** is what the service answered; a **mode** is which view is shown.

| body | control | kind | state | shared today |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| sun | sunObserverNote | JLabel | observer | read from Place and Time when the dialog opens, comes to the front, or on Update |
| sun | This instant / Over a range | JRadioButton | mode | no: each dialog's own |
| sun | sunRangeStart | JTextField | draft | no: seeded from the observer once, then the dialog's own |
| sun | sunRangeEnd | JTextField | draft | no: seeded from the observer once, then the dialog's own |
| sun | sunRangeStep | JComboBox | draft | no |
| sun | Compute | JButton | action: compute the draft | no |
| sun | Update from Place and Time | JButton | action: re-read the observer, recompute | no |
| sun | sunStatus | JLabel | result: rows or a refusal | no |
| sun | sunTable | JTable, 8 columns | result | no: the dialog's own model |
| sun | Close | JButton | furniture | the dialog's |
| moon | moonObserverNote | JLabel | observer | read from Place and Time when the dialog opens, comes to the front, or on Update |
| moon | This instant / Over a range | JRadioButton | mode | no: each dialog's own |
| moon | moonRangeStart | JTextField | draft | no: seeded from the observer once, then the dialog's own |
| moon | moonRangeEnd | JTextField | draft | no: seeded from the observer once, then the dialog's own |
| moon | moonRangeStep | JComboBox | draft | no |
| moon | Compute | JButton | action: compute the draft | no |
| moon | Update from Place and Time | JButton | action: re-read the observer, recompute | no |
| moon | moonStatus | JLabel | result: rows or a refusal | no |
| moon | moonTable | JTable, 11 columns | result | no: the dialog's own model |
| moon | Close | JButton | furniture | the dialog's |

Neither dialog persists anything (ruled on #400), and neither is told when Place and
Time changes: the observer is pulled, never pushed. The chart-visibility switches
(View ▸ Sun on the chart, Moon on the chart) are `SolarSystemModule` flags with
**no notification seam**: the menu tick is the item's own, and nothing follows the
module. Toggling opens no table; opening a table toggles nothing.

### The columns

| body | columns | packed viewport |
|---|---|---:|
| sun | instant, ra, dec, longitude, altitude, azimuth, distance, diameter | 760 px |
| moon | instant, ra, dec, altitude, azimuth, distance, diameter, illuminated, phase, elongation, litside | 1465 px |

A 360 px companion is narrower than either viewport, so a table there scrolls
sideways or the one row of the instant view is shown as lines.

## Reproduced: a table does not follow Place and Time until asked

The production Sun content over an observer supplier, headless. Place and Time moved
a day; the table still showed `2026-03-20 21:33` (as before: `2026-03-20 21:33`) until `update()` - the dialog's
Update, or its coming to the front - after which it showed `2026-03-21 21:33`.

The dialog reads when it opens, comes to the front, or on Update (ruled on #400). A group
in the Controller never comes to the front on its own, so without a rule of its own it
would show the last result for as long as the Controller stays open.

## Access letters in one window

Each dialog is its own window, so its letters are its own. In the Controller every
group shares one window with every other section. The letters the two table contents
declare, per language, and the Controller controls already using them:

| language | letter | Sun and Moon both use it for | already used in the Controller by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `en` | T | This instant | Title block |
| `en` | R | Over a range | Restore Defaults |
| `en` | C | Compute | Centre on zenith, Globular clusters |
| `en` | U | Update from Place and Time | Nebulae |
| `en` | L | Close | Deep-sky labels |
| `en` | F | From (UTC) | Flamsteed numbers, Constellation figures |
| `en` | O | To (UTC) | Open clusters |
| `en` | V | Every | — |
| `nb-NO` | D | Dette tidspunktet | Dyphimmelobjekter |
| `nb-NO` | T | Over et tidsrom | Tittelfelt |
| `nb-NO` | B | Beregn | Stjernebildegrenser, Svart himmel |
| `nb-NO` | O | Oppdater fra Sted og tid | Åpne stjernehoper |
| `nb-NO` | L | Lukk | Navn på dyphimmelobjekter |
| `nb-NO` | F | Fra (UTC) | Flamsteed-numre, Stjernebildefigurer |
| `nb-NO` | I | Til (UTC) | — |
| `nb-NO` | H | Hvert | Matematisk horisont |

Two groups with the same letters in one window is itself a collision: Swing picks
whichever registered first. The Controller's groups therefore carry no access letters
of their own (the dialogs keep theirs), or distinct ones.
