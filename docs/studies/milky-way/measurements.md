### The seam

- **3 of 202 rings wrap** in the atlas's frame (a step of more than 180 degrees between neighbouring points).
- After unwrapping, **202 of 202 rings are continuous** and 0 are not.
- The source's own frame is no safer: four rings cross its ±180 meridian, with eight steps between them. There is no frame in which nothing wraps, so the importer must unwrap rather than choose.

## The palette

Near-white washes are compared by lightness (CIE L*), not by contrast ratio: at this end of the scale every ratio sits near 1:1 and separates nothing. A difference of about 1 L* is the least the eye finds in two large flat areas; below that a level is decorative.
