# The chart toolbar's controls in the companion, measured before they move

Sprint 41, issue #449. Generated by
`juranometria.tool.CleanChartStudyMain` from the compiled application.

Every mock-up holds the production toolbar's own controls, lifted out of a bar
composed through `AtlasChrome` from a stored language, and the production companion
section heading. The section titles are study words, marked below. How tall and wide
the words are drawn is one machine's answer, in `platform.md` beside this.

**13 controls on the bar.** Every one is classified above; a control the study has not
classified fails this generator. A label answers "focusable" with Swing's default, yes,
but the layout focus policy skips a component with no focused-key bindings, so neither
label is a keyboard stop. The Emphasis button answers **no**: it is attached after the
bar re-asserts its buttons' focusability, and the look and feel takes it away again -
the one control on the bar a keyboard cannot reach.

## The chart window without its toolbar

The window's layout is `BorderLayout`: the bar north, the chart centre, the
Inspector east. Hiding the bar gives the chart the bar's own height and nothing
else; the window keeps its size, and the view state - centre, field, magnitude,
target, projection - is the controller's and is not consulted by the layout.

| | chart x, y | chart width × height | view state |
|---|---|---|---|
| toolbar shown | 0, 29 | 900 × 700 | unchanged |
| toolbar hidden | 0, 0 | 900 × 729 | unchanged |
| shown again | 0, 29 | 900 × 700 | as before |

The chart grows by exactly the bar's height and moves up by it; the bar's height is
this machine's, in `platform.md`.

## Where the companion goes, on two displays

`CompanionPlacement.place` over the screens a desktop reports: a laptop display
(1512 × 868 usable below a 33 px menu bar) and an external monitor (2560 × 1440,
placed to its right and above, as macOS arranges a taller monitor). The companion's
remembered rectangle is used when its heading strip is on a screen that exists;
otherwise it goes beside the chart window's trailing edge on that window's
screen, over the edge when there is no room. This is arithmetic the suite already
holds; what the desktop does when a monitor goes away is the owner's journey.

| case | companion placed at | on | beside or over |
|---|---|---|---|
| one screen, both windows on it | 1140, 60 360 × 814 | the laptop display | where it was |
| chart maximised on the external monitor, companion on the laptop | 1140, 60 360 × 814 | the laptop display | where it was |
| chart maximised on the external monitor, companion remembered beside it | 3700, -400 360 × 814 | the external monitor | where it was |
| the external monitor disconnected, companion last seen on it | 846, 75 360 × 814 | the laptop display | over the chart's trailing edge |
| the external monitor reconnected, companion last seen on the laptop | 1140, 60 360 × 814 | the laptop display | where it was |
| nothing remembered, chart maximised on the external monitor | 3712, -458 360 × 814 | the external monitor | over the chart's trailing edge |
