# Whether the globe's grid needs less of itself near the limb

The grid alone on empty ground, walked around circles of the disc. A grid is useful
while a reader can see between its lines, so what is reported is the clear ground
between one line and the next - not the ink, which cannot tell a few fat lines
from a hundred hairlines.

    radius    sky out
     0.20      11.5°
     0.50      30.0°
     0.80      53.1°
     0.90      64.2°
     0.95      71.8°
     0.99      81.9°

What the grid does along those circles - how many lines cross them, how much
ink they carry and what ground is left between one line and the next - is
counted from a rendering, and is in docs/studies/globe-grid/platform.md.
