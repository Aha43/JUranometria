# How much sky a hemisphere can carry

Ink is the atlas's own measure - a pixel that is not the ground - taken over bands of the
disc by plane radius, because a globe's scale collapses toward its limb and a single
number over the whole page hides where it fails first.

What the bands hold, which is the whole difficulty:

  band           of paper     of sky
  middle            25.0%      13.4%
  limb band         19.0%      43.6%
  whole disc       100.0%     100.0%

Ink twice over: the page as the atlas draws it, and the same page with nothing but
marks - so the furniture's floor can be told from what the stars add.
