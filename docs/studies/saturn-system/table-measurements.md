Chart position is topocentric astrometric ICRS/J2000; altitude and azimuth are apparent and airless, azimuth from north through east; distance is observer to the light-time-corrected centre of Saturn (not the barycentre); the apparent diameter is across the equator and from pole to pole of the projected figure at the ring opening; the north pole's position angle is of date, from celestial north through east. The rings' opening is the true geometric angle - the observer's planetocentric latitude over Saturn's pole - with the face seen; the opening to the Sun is the Sun's, as Saturn sees it, and the unlit face is named when the two are on opposite sides of the ring plane; the rings' size is the A Ring's outer edge across the major and the minor axis. A moon's offsets are Horizons' apparent differential coordinates, e and w for east and west, n and s for north and south; its state is from its disc against Saturn's flattened figure and its place against the same figure's shadow, in Jupiter's precedence. The words are the ones ruled on #574, held here in English until the reader's table carries them.
