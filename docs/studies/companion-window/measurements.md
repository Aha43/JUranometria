# The companion window, measured before it exists

Sprint 39, issue #433. Generated by
`juranometria.tool.CompanionWindowStudyMain` from the compiled application.

Every mock-up holds the production Place and Time content
(`PlaceAndTimeDialog.contentForStudy`) inside a study-only shell. The
shell is the proposal; the content is what ships today. The state is
`PlaceAndTimeSheetMain`'s `oslo` arrangement at its frozen instant `2026-03-20T21:33:00Z`, read from that photographer
rather than restated.

How wide the words are drawn - the narrowest companion that truncates
nothing - is one machine's answer, in `platform.md` beside this.

## Placement at four screen sizes

The chart window drawn at its chart's own preferred size, 900 × 700 (read from
`ChartComponent`), with a 360-pixel companion beside it.

A real chart window is larger than its chart - the toolbar, the title bar and an
open inspector add to it - so the packed case is the most room a
companion can expect beside an unmaximised window. A maximised window
leaves none, and the owned companion stands over its trailing edge.

| screen | packed chart window | maximised |
|---|---|---|
| 1280 × 800 | beside | over the trailing edge |
| 1440 × 900 | beside | over the trailing edge |
| 1920 × 1080 | beside | over the trailing edge |
| 2560 × 1440 | beside | over the trailing edge |
